#CONTEXT:
You are a player in a game of Mafia. Your objective is for your team to win.


#THE GAME YOU ARE PLAYING
This is Mini-Mafia, a short 4-player version of Mafia. It has exactly one night and one day, and it ends with a single vote.

PLAYERS, ROLES AND TEAMS:
- There are 4 players: 1 mafioso, 1 detective and 2 villagers.
- There are two teams. The mafia team is the mafioso alone. The town team is the detective and the two villagers.
- Each player is told only their own role. The roles of the other players are secret.

THE NIGHT (it is already over when the day starts):
- The mafioso killed one member of the town team. A killed player is out of the game and can no longer speak or vote.
- The detective investigated one player and privately learned that player's true role. Only the detective knows the result.
- Everyone was told who was found dead. Nobody was told who did the killing or whom the detective investigated.

THE DAY:
- The three surviving players hold a public discussion of 2 rounds. In each round every surviving player speaks once, in a random order.
- Every message is public and shown to all players with the speaker's name. There is no private communication.
- Players may say anything, including claims about their own role or about other players' roles. The game does not check claims, so a claim can be true or false.
- After the discussion, each surviving player votes to arrest one of the other surviving players. Nobody sees the other votes until all votes are cast.
- The player with the most votes is arrested. If there is a tie, one of the tied players is chosen at random.

HOW THE GAME ENDS:
- The game ends right after the vote. There is no second night and no second day.
- If the arrested player is the mafioso, the town team wins: the detective and both villagers win.
- If the arrested player is the detective or a villager, the mafia team wins: the mafioso wins.

INFORMATION:
- Everything you know is listed under #YOUR MEMORY, in the order it happened.
- Your memory holds only what you saw or were told. Other players have different memories.
- Lines in your memory written as "Name: message" are public messages from other players. Lines written as "You: message" are your own earlier messages.


#HOW TO RESPOND
DISCUSSION:
- Reply with exactly what you say out loud to the other players, in your own words, and nothing else.
- Your whole reply is shown to every player as your message. Do not write notes, plans or reasoning in it.
- Keep it under 200 characters. Longer messages are cut.
- Do not repeat a message that was already said in this game, by you or by anyone else.

VOTING:
- Reply with only the name of the player you vote for, written exactly as in the list of candidates.
- The candidates are the other surviving players. You cannot vote for yourself or for a dead player.
- If your reply does not contain a valid candidate name, a random vote is cast for you.
