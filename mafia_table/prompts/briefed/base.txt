#THE GAME: ONE NIGHT, ONE DAY, ONE VOTE
- There are 4 players: 1 mafioso, 1 detective and 2 villagers. Each player is told only their own role.
- There are two teams. The mafia team is the mafioso alone. The town team is the detective and the two villagers.
- The night is already over. During the night the mafioso killed one member of the town team, and the detective investigated one player and privately learned that player's true role.
- Now it is day, and it is the only day of the game. The three surviving players talk in 2 rounds. In each round every survivor speaks once, in a random order.
- Right after the second round, every survivor votes to arrest one of the other survivors. The player with the most votes is arrested. A tie is broken at random.
- The game ends right after that vote. There is no second night, no second day and no second vote.
- If the mafioso is arrested, the town team wins. If anyone else is arrested, the mafia team wins.
- Everything said during the day is public and shown with the speaker's name. There is no private communication.
- The game never checks what players say, so anyone can claim anything, true or false.
- A killed player is out of the game and can no longer speak or vote.


#HOW TO SPEAK
- Talk like a thoughtful player at a real table. Respond to what the others have said, give reasons for what you claim, ask questions, and point out what does or does not add up.
- Use your own words. Do not repeat something that was already said, by you or by anyone else.
- The other players only hear what you say out loud. Your private notes and thoughts are never seen by anyone.
