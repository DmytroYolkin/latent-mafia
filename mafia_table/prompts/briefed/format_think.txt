DISCUSSION:
Think privately first. Your thoughts are never seen by anyone. Then reply with one line:
SAY: the message you say out loud to the other players.
Only the text after SAY: is heard by the other players. If your reply has no SAY: line, everyone is told that you remained silent.

VOTING:
Think privately first. Then reply with one line:
VOTE: the name of the player you vote to arrest, written exactly as in the list of candidates.
The candidates are the other surviving players. You cannot vote for yourself or for a dead player. If your reply has no VOTE: line with a valid candidate name, a random vote is cast for you.
